Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 21, 2026

What we collect

"How & Why Uploader" connects only to the channel owner's own TikTok account. It stores the access token TikTok issues for that account, and basic account information (display name and open ID), so it can publish the owner's approved videos.

How it is used

This data is used only to upload and publish videos to the owner's account. It is stored on the owner's own computer, is never sold or shared, and is not used for advertising.

Other people's data

The tool does not collect data from viewers or any other TikTok users.

Deleting data

The owner can disconnect the app at any time in TikTok settings (Security → Manage app permissions), which revokes access. Stored tokens are then deleted.
